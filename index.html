02Research

Conservation versus Trajectory Accuracy in Direct Gravitational N-Body Integration

Computational physics benchmark | Software release v1.2.0 | Zenodo DOI | MIT licence

A reproducible benchmark comparing Euler, velocity-Verlet, classical fourth-order Runge-Kutta (RK4) and fourth-order Yoshida (Yoshida4) for direct Newtonian N-body integration, with SciPy's adaptive DOP853 as a numerical baseline.

The study separates two questions that are often confused: how well a method conserves energy, and how accurate its trajectory is. It uses analytical Kepler solutions, timestep refinement, 1,000-orbit eccentric integrations (eccentricity 0.9), a figure-eight return test, and Sun-Earth, Sun-Earth-Mars and binary-star systems. Diagnostics include relative energy error, angular-momentum error, position error, centre-of-mass drift, convergence order and computational cost.

Key finding: good energy conservation does not guarantee small trajectory error over long runs, so methods should not be ranked on energy error alone.
